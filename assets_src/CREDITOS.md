- cuchillo1-2, recoger, desenvainar ← knife_slice*, draw_knife* (Kenney, RPG Audio, s03)
- explosion, explosionGrande, retumbo ← explosion_crunch_000/004, low_frequency_explosion_000 (Kenney, Sci-fi Sounds, s04)
- hueso1-2, metal1-2, balaMetal1-2, chispas1, choque, cristal, emp ← Break_*, Impact_*, SmallImpact_*, ElectroZap, BlastImpact_02, BreakGlass_01, EMP_03 (CoMiGo, Bleeps and Bloops, s01)
- servo, chispas2 ← Retro Charge StereoUP 12, Retro Electric Short 03 (Kronbits, 200 Free SFX, s05)

- smg1-2 ← PPSh P_22P / P_34P
